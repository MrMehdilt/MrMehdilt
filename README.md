### 👋 About me

I'm **Mehdi**, a full-stack developer turned entrepreneur, based in **Algiers 🇩🇿**.
I've led dev teams, and today I build at the intersection of **code, education and business**.

- 🎓 Co-founder of **Code & Sens** — tech school, coworking & incubator
- 🚀 Founder of **Binnovant** — business digitalization
- 🗺️ Building **Rondonna** — local tourism app for Algeria
- 🎨 Also into design & 3D (Figma, Adobe, Blender)
- 💬 Arabic · French · English
